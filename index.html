Our story

We grind the masala every morning at six

Chef Arjun Mehta opened Tandoor & Tamarind in 2012 with one clay oven and his grandmother's spice box. The oven is still lit before sunrise, and every masala is ground in house, never bought in a packet.

The menu moves between the north's smoky tandoor and the south's banana leaf meals, because that is how the family eats at home.

Ground dailyWhole spices, roasted and pounded each morning
